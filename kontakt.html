About Animedel

Animedel is an online shop offering accessories for dogs and cats, plus textiles and accessories with Animedel designs. We sell to customers in Norway and also deliver to the EU/EEA and Peru. Many products are printed to order by European production partners to avoid overproduction. More about us (in Norwegian and Spanish): Om oss.
